# Review notes

- Data substitution: the paper's four years of manual 6-hourly readings from a Cumilla (Bangladesh) nursery pond are not public, so the task ships a deterministic synthetic record (seed 20260801, environment/data/gen_inputs.py, deleted from the image after the CSV is written at build time). It reproduces the paper's qualitative regime: strong annual cycle, air-water correlation weakening in winter, ~5-8% of winter readings below 20 degC, ~3-6% missing manual entries, 5,844 rows x 6 columns. The grader regenerates the identical record from tests/gold_gen.py and flags any tampering with the input CSV.
- Shortcut probes considered: (a) the same-hour-yesterday persistence baseline is nearly optimal in this domain (the paper reports MAE 1.117 vs 1.116 degC for XGBoost), so one graded constraint requires the 6-h MAE to beat that baseline by >=15%; (b) a constant 'no alert' classifier scores high accuracy but F1=0, blocked by the F1/sensitivity floors plus an alert-rate window (0.4x-3x the true cold count); (c) inverting the data-generating process for an oracle forecast is blocked by an intrinsic-noise floor (MAE >= 0.25 degC at every lead and F1 <= 0.995), and by the generator being removed from the image after data creation.
- Tool chain: python:3.11-slim with numpy 1.26.4, pandas 2.2.2, scipy 1.13.1, scikit-learn 1.4.2 and xgboost 2.0.3 (the paper's own stack). The grader does not reimplement a domain binary; it recomputes labels, baselines and reference performance with sklearn on the protected record.
- Grading is calibrated: MAE floors (reference MAE x 1.15 + 0.03 degC) and F1/sensitivity floors (reference - 0.10) are derived at verification time by a hidden reference implementation that follows the same 2022-2023 / 2024 / 2025 protocol, so the absolute numbers need not match the paper's Bangladesh record. Partial credit is the weighted fraction of 14 independent checks (total weight 17.5); no nested objects are written to reward.json.
- Known limitations: split discipline (fit on 2022-2023 only) cannot be observed directly by the grader and is enforced indirectly through metric honesty, coverage, anti-leakage plausibility and the threshold-consistency check; boundary-zone calibration is graded only at the 6-h lead; the synthetic record inherits the paper's single-pond scope, so generalization to other ponds is untested as the authors themselves note.
