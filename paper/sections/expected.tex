\section{Expected Results and Contributions}
\label{sec:expected}

\subsection{Contributions}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{First automated pipeline} from life-science papers to
        \harbor-formatted executable environments.
  \item \textbf{Open-source environment set}: 100+ executable, verifiable
        \harbor\ tasks spanning computational biology subfields.
  \item \textbf{Causal evaluation}: evidence of whether automatically generated
        environments improve model capabilities on held-out scientific
        benchmarks.
  \item \textbf{Generalizable methodology}: a pipeline architecture extensible
        to physics, chemistry, and earth sciences.
  \item \textbf{Community infrastructure}: complementary to DrugDiscoveryBench,
        Terminal-Bench-Science, and BioAgent Gym.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Anticipated findings}

The following are \emph{expectations}, not measurements. Each is registered in
Appendix~\ref{app:claims} with status \texttt{planned} together with the control
arm it must be measured against; none of them is reported as a result anywhere
in this manuscript.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Stage 1}: construction success rate of 60--80\% for
        computational biology papers with available code; expert approval rate
        of 70--85\% for the reviewed subset (\cid{P1}, \cid{P3}).
  \item \textbf{Stage 2}: replication of RSI-Forge's $\geq$84\% first-attempt
        improvement rate in life-science environments (\cid{P5}).
  \item \textbf{Stage 3}: model differences exceeding harness differences,
        consistent with HarnessOpt-Bench \cite{harnessoptbench}, unless
        domain-specific toolchains (e.g.\ Bioconductor, samtools) dominate
        (\cid{P7}).
  \item \textbf{Stage 4}: statistically significant improvement on held-out
        benchmarks for RLVR and RaR training compared to no-environment
        controls, with the strongest gains in tasks matching the training
        subfields and partial transfer to cross-subfield tasks (\cid{P8},
        \cid{P9}).
\end{itemize}
